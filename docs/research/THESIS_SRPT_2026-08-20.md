## 1. The claim

Sarepta at ~$19.40 is priced as a terminally declining franchise, but the Q2 2026
filing shows a stabilized ~$1.6–1.9B-revenue business running at roughly
cash-flow breakeven at its trough, with three dated regulatory options the price
does not pay for; expected re-rate to $30+ (~+55%) within 12 months, absent a new
ELEVIDYS safety event or an FDA rejection of the PMO traditional-approval
filings.

## 3. Who is on the other side

Sellers of an −84% collapse who believe the DMD franchise is structurally broken:
the regulator has been an adversary on the record (platform designation revoked,
full suspension requested); management's own H2 guide implies flat-to-−18%
half-over-half; safety tail risk is unhedgeable (two deaths, boxed warning — one
more ambulatory event ends the thesis overnight); securities class action and
derivative suit pending. Consensus, judging by a ~3x EPS beat moving the stock
only +5%: "trough is real but nothing grows." This thesis takes the other side of
exactly that.

## 4. Worth if right / cost if wrong

105.4M basic shares → mcap ~$2.05B at $19.40; EV ≈ $2.15B → EV/Sales ≈ 1.2x on
guided $1.75–1.9B. If right: 2.0–2.5x EV/Sales on ~$1.7B → $31–39 (+60–100%);
on 122.3M diluted, $28–35 (≥+45%). If wrong: PDUFA rejection → ~$12 (−38%); new
ambulatory safety event → $8–10 (−50–60%). At 50/50 on regulatory outcomes the
asymmetry is modestly positive; the bet is that stabilization (items 4–6) is the
base case, not a coin flip.

## 5. Invalidation conditions (exit rules if this becomes a position)

1. ELEVIDYS U.S. quarterly net product revenue < $75M in any quarter. Dead.
2. Any new confirmed acute liver failure/death in an ambulatory ELEVIDYS patient.
   Dead, immediately.
3. FDA Complete Response or negative adcomm on the AMONDYS/VYONDYS sNDAs (PDUFA
   2027-02-28), or any move to withdraw a PMO accelerated approval. Dead.
4. ENDEAVOR Cohort 8 shows acute liver injury despite sirolimus — kills the
   non-ambulatory leg; degrade to trough-value only, cut size.
5. PMO revenue down >10% sequentially two consecutive quarters. Dead.
6. Cash below ~$700M without an explained one-time use. Dead.
