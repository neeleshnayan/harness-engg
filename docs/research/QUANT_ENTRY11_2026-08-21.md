## Headline: THREE FAILS, AND THE BAND DOES NOT FLIP ON COST

Gate v4.1 fails the candidate at all three slippage assumptions, three
failure sentences each — **and none of the three is a cost failure.** The
cost-sensitive criterion (beat the equal-weight SPY/TLT basket) PASSES at
every slip: returns 49.795 / 46.05 / 42.456% against a 40.75% benchmark
over 5.47 years. Measured cost sensitivity (linear fit on the three runs):
`total_return% = 51.605 − 1.835 × slip_bps` — **the excess over the basket
dies at 5.92 bps/side**, just outside the ordered band and squarely inside
the fund's own cost measurement uncertainty (the 5.95 global constant vs
the validator's outlier-corrected 3.34). The proposal understated its own
cost sensitivity by ~40% (0.336%/yr per bps measured vs 0.24 predicted).

The three failure sentences, decomposed honestly:

1. **PSR 0.95/0.75/0.58% vs a 65% floor — an instrument artifact for this
   shape.** The rule is always fully invested in a 50/50 basket, so its
   book Sharpe IS the basket's (~0.15); no overlay on a fully-invested
   basket can clear a total-book PSR floor regardless of the overlay's
   quality. v4.1's PSR is a beta filter here, not a luck test.
2. **"Cost robustness was never measured" — structural.** The spec forbids
   a sweep ("no sweep, one declared parameter"), and a one-point grid
   cannot interpolate a breakeven. Every declared-cost candidate will
   carry this sentence until the gate gains vocabulary for it.
3. **Walk-forward 2 of 4 folds retained — THE genuine finding.** Fold 3
   (2025-10→2026-02) was negative at every slip. This is the shape of an
   effect being competed away and matches the mechanism's own
   pre-registered F4 falsifier (post-publication half a coin flip). Two
   folds in the 1/3bps candidates were engine-timeout absences (300s cap
   under three concurrent candidates), not strategy properties; the 5bps
   candidate completed all four and still reads 2-of-4.
