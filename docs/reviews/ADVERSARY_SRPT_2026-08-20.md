## The five grounds

### 1. The "re-rate" is sector beta — the fourth beta-in-skill's-clothing find

The memo read SRPT's +23.9% (Aug 5→19) as the market absorbing the Q2 beat.
Measured: beta to XBI = 1.60 (250d, pre-event window). Beta×XBI explains +17.3%
of it; the residual vs IBB is **negative**. The single biggest day (Aug 19,
+9.49%) has an idiosyncratic residual of **+0.06%** and no 8-K behind it — a
biotech sector day. The thesis's mechanism (multiple expansion) IS the sector
risk-appetite factor, unhedged and undiscussed.

### 2. The valuation base is ~31% non-recurring or zero-margin

Of H1 collab revenue $472.9M, **$365.0M is one-time** (the $325.0M Roche option
lapse + $40.0M milestone, both stated in the 10-Q Roche note). The raised
$550–600M collab guide is largely **zero-margin pass-through** — the CFO's next
sentence on the call the memo cited: contract-manufacturing revenue rises with
"a roughly equivalent increase in cost of goods." On the sustainable base the
stock is ~25% more expensive than the memo claims (EV/S ~1.4–1.5x, not 1.2x).
Also: the entry price was wrong ($19.73 actual close vs $19.40 claimed), and the
collapse citation misdated ($119.33 is the 2025-01-15 close — a lookback-window
artifact; the real drawdown is −90% from there, −92.7% from the true peak).

### 3. Dead on arrival by its own exit rule

The memo's invalidation #1: ELEVIDYS < $75M/quarter = Dead. Take management's
guidance midpoint ($1.25B products) and the memo's OWN premise (PMO flat):
implied H2 ELEVIDYS = **$65.8M/quarter — below the memo's own kill threshold in
both remaining quarters.** The thesis survives its own exit rule only at the top
of a range management just cut. And management guided the "stable ~$100M/qtr"
line DOWN on the very call the memo sourced ("ELEVIDYS revenue in the third
quarter to trend lower than Q2").

### 4. One of six invalidation conditions is monitorable

The product-split triggers need a table the corpus does not store (extractor
caps ~6 obs/filing); the three event triggers (safety event, CRL, Cohort 8 ALI)
need a news feed the fund does not have — and cannot have while "no agent runs
without a human trigger" holds. Only the cash floor is checkable from fund data.
The same failure that killed the VRP proposal, reproduced.

### 5. Position fit is independently disqualifying

SRPT's measured worst day is **−54.7% (2025-06-16, this name, this mechanism)**.
At the 20% position cap that is an **11%-of-NAV overnight gap** no exit rule can
intercept — the memo's "safety event → −50–60%" downside is gap risk described
as if it were a stop-loss. Realized vol 81.6% annualised; 18 of 399 days moved
>±10%.
