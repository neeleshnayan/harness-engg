# THE OPERATOR GUIDE — the firm's book

**Chartered 2026-08-27 by the CEO, verbatim:** *"what I want you to think
through is how we compound our understanding of markets with each run. Most
top firms build expertise over time and operational muscle which is what we
need too. Our research, the essence, the learnings, the mistakes and
corrections all need to compound into an operator guide for us."*

## The problem this solves, stated honestly

The firm's memory is append-only and excellent: seat STATEs, findings docs,
the day log, archives, the ticket highway. **A log compounds by growing;
expertise compounds by distilling.** Nobody can read 1,100 lines of builder
history before acting — so lessons the firm has paid for get re-derived, and
the paying was the expensive part. The Guide is the distillation: small,
curated, organized by TOPIC not by date, and read BEFORE acting rather than
archaeologically after.

## The four books

| book | question it answers | seeded from |
|---|---|---|
| `MARKETS.md` | What do we KNOW about how markets work — measured, dated, falsifiable | findings docs, seat STATEs, belt results |
| `OPERATIONS.md` | How do we do X safely — runbooks, each born from an incident | cto.md, incident record |
| `FAILURES.md` | The mistake classes we have paid for, and the guard now standing on each | post-mortems, retractions, kills of our own beliefs |
| `EDGES.md` | What we tried in the market, what it taught, what decayed | Ed's menu, gate verdicts, kill records |

## The compounding protocol — how entries get in, stay honest, and get read

1. **An entry is a CLAIM with its receipt**: the statement, the measurement
   that grounds it, the date, the citation into the record, and what would
   falsify it. Working protocol 1 applied to knowledge. No receipt, no entry.
2. **Distillation is a scheduled act, not a hope.** Donna's EoD run gains a
   GUIDE section: candidate entries from the day's STATEs, incidents and
   verdicts, proposed as diffs. The chair reviews and commits — same shape as
   BINDS review. Weekly, the chair prunes: an entry nothing has cited in its
   briefs for a month is a candidate for the archive, not for deletion.
3. **The Guide is CONSUMED at dispatch or it is decoration.** Brief
   composition cites the relevant book sections (a quant brief on crypto
   carries MARKETS.md#crypto; every builder brief carries OPERATIONS.md
   links). Consumption is measurable — the same adoption discipline as the
   `## TICKETS` grammar.
4. **Contradiction flags, never overwrites.** A new measurement that
   disagrees with an entry does not edit it silently — the entry gains a
   CONTESTED mark and a re-decision routes through the desk. The
   findings-doc rule, applied to knowledge.
5. **Small is the feature.** The books stay readable in one sitting. The
   record holds everything; the Guide holds the essence. Link, never
   duplicate — the day log's own law.
6. **The Guide feeds the seat pages** (lessons & growth section) and,
   eventually, the demo: *"here is the book our machine wrote about markets,
   every line with its receipt"* is an artifact no fund this size has.
