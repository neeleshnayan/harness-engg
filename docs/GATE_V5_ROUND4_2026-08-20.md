## 1. The premia criterion — vol-matched paired MPPM, with a refusal mode

Ground 1's kill: paired test-leg Sharpe certified a fair-priced insurance
seller at 72–75% against a 12% true-positive rate, because Sharpe is the
statistic option-like payoffs maximise. The replacement is the
manipulation-proof performance measure (Goetzmann/Ingersoll/Spiegel/Welch,
RFS 2007): a CRRA certainty-equivalent growth rate, **ρ = 5**, with the
strategy levered to the benchmark's realised vol over the same window
before comparison. Because both legs then sit at the same vol, the Gaussian
variance penalty cancels in the pair — ρ moves only skew/tail
discrimination (measured: the ρ sweep moves the seller columns, not the
defensive TP).

Four legs, all required (`rule_premia_r4`):

0. **Structure guard**: the strategy stream's 21-day variance ratio must
   sit under `VR_MAX = 2.0`. Discovered mid-round: a zero-mean AR(1) with
   ρ=.98 realises decade-long drifts of ±30%/yr (the sample mean of a
   persistent process has enormous variance), and grid selection picks the
   up-wanderers — passing draws in the probe carried **+24 to +95%/yr of
   realised drift at 9–10% daily vol**. A realisation that genuinely
   delivered Sharpe ~1 for ten years cannot be rejected by ANY
   backward-looking performance statistic; the only honest verdict for a
   high-persistence stream is **CANNOT TELL** (a refusal, not a pass, and
   in the live gate a named refusal reason). iid-like streams sit at
   VR ≈ 1; the AR(1) null sits at ~19. Measured effect: AR(1) pass rate
   17–18% → **0.0%** at every ρ, defensive TP unchanged.
1. Majority of measurable per-fold TEST-leg paired comparisons (daily
   scale), fold measurability per §3.
2. Full-history daily-scale comparison above the margin — a rare loss
   cannot dodge the full sample: if it happened at all, the CRRA average
   carries it.
3. Full-history 21-day-aggregated comparison above the margin — the
   horizon where autocorrelation shows up in the vol that matching must
   respect (daily-scale MPPM is structurally blind to it).

## 5. The claim-type taxonomy (Ground 4), one paragraph

A claim type is a DECLARATION made by the artifact before results exist
(constitution: premia and alpha carry different success criteria). The
gate judges ONLY the declared claim; the other column is **n/a —
undeclared**, neither a pass nor a false positive. A strictly-better-Sharpe
alpha strategy that would also satisfy the premia definition is judged as
what it claimed to be. All FP/TP aggregates in this doc apply that
convention; alpha_S0.6/alpha_S1.0 premia cells are excluded rather than
counted against either rule.

## 6. Honest limits — stated with numbers, not hidden

- **Irreducible blindness**: no statistic can see a risk absent from the
  sample. P(zero loss events) at n=630: 12.2% for (1/300, 15%), 53.2% for
  (1/1000, 30%); at n=2520: 0.02% and 8.0%. The measured residual passes
  sit AT these blind rates (sv_300_15_b0: 11.4% at 630 → 0.0% at depth;
  sv_1000_30: 8.9% ≈ 8.0% blind). The remedy is depth and declared-risk
  disclosure, not a better statistic.
- **sv_300_15_b1 passes 12.6% at depth** (vs TP 42.1%, ratio 3.3:1). A
  beta-1 seller's lever is ~1, so its rare loss is not amplified by vol
  matching; one-event decades squeak past margin 2. Margin 3 buys 9.8%
  at TP 33.6% — the trade is on the table for the CEO.
- **NEW HOLE, found by this round's own depth run: `v5_alpha` certifies
  the slow-bleed insurance seller at 46.9%** (sv_1000_30_b0, n=2520
  scaled). The alpha rule's retention arithmetic has no tail sensitivity:
  a 7.6%/yr premium with a 1-in-1000-day loss retains beautifully in
  almost every fold. The premia rule catches the same process at 8.9%
  (CRRA + full-sample legs). Named remedy, to be measured before v5
  ships: the structure guard (leg 0) plus a full-sample tail check
  extended to ALPHA claims — an insurance seller declaring alpha must not
  be judged by a rule blind to its tail. Until that is measured, this
  46.9% is an open, quantified defect of the alpha criterion at depth.
- The generator remains beta×bench+idio (plus AR(1), events, and regime
  switches); it calibrates null train-return LEVELS to the belt (r3 §1
  correction, which survived its kill) but is still a model. The belt's
  own nulls remain the final calibration target.

## §8 Correction and reframing (2026-08-20, validator audit 5fc56190 — new section, doc unedited above)

The validator's fold-geometry audit found, and the CTO reproduced on the
shipped function the same hour, that **this doc's fold generator is not the
belt's**. `_folds(n)` in the audit script PACKS folds across the whole
history (4/12/27 at 630/1260/2520 days); the belt's
`window_for_strategy` (walkforward.py:223-228) fixes reach-back at
`train + test·(K+1)` and lets the history floor only CLIP — measured:
hold-3 gets 5 folds with IDENTICAL test-leg dates at a 2024-02-26 floor
and a 2016-08-22 floor; hold-21 gains exactly the one fold the current
floor was clipping (4 → 5) and then caps forever.

Consequences, stated plainly:

1. **§3's history table is a measurement of a PROPOSED fold generator, not
   the shipped one.** Through the shipped `window_for`, fold counts at
   630/1260/2520 days are 4/5/5 — the 7.1% → 34.0% fixed-floor loosening
   cannot occur on the belt as it stands, and §3 must not be cited as
   evidence about current behavior.
2. **§7's sequencing claim was wrong as written**: the 10-year backfill,
   through the current window function, buys ~zero additional folds and
   ~zero out-of-sample coverage — it extends TRAIN legs only. The "premia
   TP 12% → 42% at depth" tables describe the gate's power IF folds grow
   with history, which requires a window-function change nothing in this
   doc had specified.
3. **The reframed package** — what v5's history limb actually is: (a) a
   window-function change: `window_for` packs folds across available
   history above the floor (capped), so history genuinely converts into
   out-of-sample evidence; (b) `available` in the scaled floor is DEFINED
   as `len(folds)` of that new generator (the validator showed the old
   doc's `available` was unresolvable — a permanent no-op under one
   reading, a hard block on 21-day holds under the other); (c) the scaled
   floor `max(4, ceil(0.6 × available))`, whose necessity §3 measures FOR
   THE NEW GENERATOR — that is what §3's table now honestly is; (d) only
   then the backfill. One versioned package, CEO-decided, adversary-read.
   Sequencing the scaled floor BEFORE the generator change would invert
   testability toward the least-covered rules (short holds sail, hold-21
   flips NOT TESTABLE — validator §4a).
4. **The statistic work is untouched**: the MPPM/VR/margin battery (§1,
   §2), the measurability fix, the class maximum, and the taxonomy do not
   depend on the fold generator's history behavior. The adversary should
   attack them as filed, and attack §3 as a proposed-generator measurement.
5. The fold-count/coverage INVERSION itself (the mechanism's D1) is
   confirmed with closed forms and is an independent axis no count floor
   fixes: span(hold-21)/span(hold-1) = 24.2× at every K — the coverage
