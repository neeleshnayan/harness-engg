## 3. Proposed v5 criteria (from the tables, chosen in the open)

1. **ALPHA claims**: beta-adjusted excess (train-leg OLS beta, out-of-sample),
   every fold measurable, `MIN_TRAIN_EXCESS = 2.0%` annualised with the
   strict-positive guard separate, retention ≥ 0.5 on annualised excess,
   strict majority of folds. Measured: nulls 4.9–8.2% (one exception below),
   alpha TP 22.8–29.6% — v4-comparable power with ~4× better discrimination.
2. **PREMIA claims**: paired test-leg Sharpe with **margin 0.5**, strict
   majority. Measured: nulls ≤3.9% rising / ≤7.0% falling; defensive premia
   TP 12.0% rising / 24.9% falling. The honest cost is stated: at n=630 the
   premia test is LOW-POWERED in rising markets — a true premia strategy
   mostly returns "not proven yet", which is the correct humility at 630
   sessions and improves with history.
3. **Fold scaling**: NOT adopted for v5 rules — measured as a no-op for
   v5_alpha (the defect the register feared is v4-specific: v4's raw floor
   shrinks the measurable subset; v5 makes every fold measurable, so more
   history TIGHTENS fixed-4 FPR, 9.1% → 0.2%). The register entry should be
   annotated at v5 adoption, not silently satisfied.
4. **The holdout leg** carries v4.1's discipline (walkforward.retention())
   on the excess scale for alpha claims — same function, excess series.

## 4. What round 3 does NOT fix, stated before the adversary finds it

- **Beta drift still leaks**: `null_drift.5-2` passes v5_alpha at **16.2%**
  (round 2 measured 14.9% — reproduced, not repaired). Per-fold train-leg
  betas track drift only between folds, not within them. Candidate fixes for
  round 4 — a beta-stability criterion (max spread of fold betas), or
  intra-fold rolling beta — need their own measurement; adopting v5 with this
  hole DOCUMENTED beats holding the whole gate for it, but that is a
  judgement the adversary and the CEO get to attack.
- **Selection statistic ≠ judging statistic**: `_sweep_summary` still picks
  winners by max RAW train return while v5 judges on excess. The model now
  INCLUDES that mismatch (grid-max on raw), so the tables carry its cost —
  but re-deriving selection on the excess scale (validator prerequisite) is
  unmeasured and open.
- **Simulated processes**, still: iid Gaussian alpha streams, constant vol,
  no costs. The real-belt null audit under v5 (validator's standing
  measurement debt) is the final falsifier once v5 is implemented.
