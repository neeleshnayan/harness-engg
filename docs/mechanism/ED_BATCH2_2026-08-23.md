## PROPOSAL P1 — Entry 11 ADVANCED: month-turn rebalancing reversal (SPY/TLT)

```yaml
  mechanism: fixed-target calendar rebalancers sell the month's winning asset into month-end;
    the pressure reverts in the first sessions of the new month; holding the winner from close
    of T to close of T+3 harvests the reversion
  counterparty: ~$20trn pension/TDF fixed-mix mandates (NBER w33554 - 17bp next-day impact,
    ~$16bn/yr transfer); they keep paying because mandate tracking, not price, drives the trade

      granularity at 24 sides/yr on a $917 book
    vol_ratio: 0.90 (PREDICTED, UNVERIFIED - estimate: 50/50-with-tilts vs an EW-BH benchmark
      that drifts equity-heavy; to be scored vs strategy equity/benchmark_curve, session clock)

    clone coming alive (measured dead: -1.33 bps/mo, t -0.19, n 282)
  cost_grid: [0, 5, 10, 15, 20]  # top point 2x the gate floor
```

**The rule, frozen (cycle-1 spec, unchanged, one free parameter):** baseline 50/50 SPY/TLT rebalanced at close of month's 3rd session; at close of T-5 read s; at close of T go 100/0 (s>0) or 0/100 (s<=0); at close of T+3 return to 50/50. Never cash, no leverage, no shorts.

Unlike Entry 21, the **most recent eras are above floor** (18.7 / 19.8) — E21 died under floor exactly where it was out of sample; this one does not.
- Decomposition: three-quarters of the effect is T->T+1 (+19.39, t=+3.45). Sign asymmetry, reported against the author: the entire effect is the s>0 (equity) leg (+43.18, t=+4.52, n=168); the bond leg is zero (+0.54, t=+0.03). The story permits it (equity flow moves equities more than bond flow moves Treasuries) but does not predict it.
- **Magnitude conditioning now PASSES**: cycle-1's 38-month inverse ordering — the test that helped defer this entry — reverses at 282 months: active by |gap| tercile **+14.83 / +34.75 / +79.95** (top t=+4.74), monotone in the direction the mechanism requires.

- Worst month: 2008-12, -467.87 bps (held TLT through the year-end squeeze). Long history testable: yes, 2003+ (282 months in hand); era structure declared above.
