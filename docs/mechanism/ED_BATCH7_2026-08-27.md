## P1 — the Ethereum staking transfer, harvested through the wrapper

**Mechanism (an accounting identity, not a forecast)**: Ethereum mints new
ETH and pays it ONLY to stakers; every non-staking holder is diluted by
exactly that issuance. **Counterparty**: the ~70% of supply that does not
stake — including the largest non-staking ETP (ETHA) itself. **The trade**:
hold the Grayscale Ethereum Mini Trust (feed symbol `ETH`, 0.15% fee,
stakes since 2025-10-06) instead of ETHA (0.25%, does not stake). Claim
type: premia. One switch, ~10bps, repaid in ~4 weeks; capacity >$1bn; no
short, no leverage, no signal, no timing.

**Measured** (526 common sessions, adjclose verified against a real
distribution): ETH-mini/ETHA pre-staking −0.107%/yr → post **+1.863%/yr**;
diff-in-diff **+1.969%/yr**. The instrument validates itself: ETHE's
pre-staking drift (−2.372%/yr) reproduces its stated 2.25% fee gap to
0.12pp; three non-staking control pairs sit at zero in both periods.

**The statistic that matters**: daily-differenced t=+0.99 (noise of
non-synchronous closing prints); **monthly-aggregated t=+4.22, IR 4.40;
sign test 10 of 11 months positive, p=0.0059**. Expected ~2.71%/yr from
disclosed yields/fees vs measured +1.86% — the gap consistent with an
unstaked redemption buffer (testable sub-prediction). Honest limits: the
same-sponsor control (ETHB) is the weakest leg (+0.807%/yr, t=+0.27); the
position's Sharpe is ETH's plus a sliver — this is not a PSR-wall claim
and does not pretend to be.

**Falsifier F1 (primary, uses no returns)**: issuer-disclosed ETH-per-share
ratio growth, mini vs ETHA, any 90 days — ≤1.000 kills it. **F2**: fewer
than 7 of the next 12 monthly active returns positive.
