## PROPOSAL — Entry 21: the Treasury auction concession. BELT-READY. Claim type: ALPHA (declared before results).

**Mechanism:** Treasury sells duration on a published calendar; primary dealers are contractually obliged to bid and must warehouse what they win. Warehousing long duration consumes regulatorily scarce balance sheet (SLR), so dealers push the secondary market cheaper in the days before the auction to buy at a discount. A payment for immediacy and inventory risk on a date everyone can see — and it does not compete away because the natural arbitrageurs ARE the constrained party.

**Measured on our own feed (2002-07-30 → 2026-08-21, n=6,055 sessions):** TLT−SHY over the 3 sessions before a 20y/30y auction = **−0.265%, t = −3.36, n=347**. Duration-ordered exactly as the mechanism requires (20y −0.438% t −2.96 → 2y +0.153); monotone in offering size; present in both halves and **STRENGTHENING** (−0.150 → −0.379 since 2019, matching the constraint tightening). Day-of-month control: +28.47%/yr, t +3.88 — not a monthly seasonal. Random-anchor matched-exposure null: **0 of 300 draws exceed the base +6.95%/yr.** Disclosed honestly for the adversary: calendar shifts near multiples of the ~21-session auction cycle DO reproduce the effect (they land back on real events); the off-cycle shifts (±10/±15/±30) are the honest placebos and they are noise.

**Counterparty:** the United States Treasury, via its primary dealers — a statutory payer with a statutory reason (Lou, Yan & Zhang, RFS 2013: 9–18 bps of auction size). **Well-known alpha, said plainly**; the persistence argument is specific: harvesting it properly requires exactly the balance sheet that is scarce, and a long-only ETF underweight is available to us and not to a constrained dealer.

**The rule (implementable without a question):** universe {EDV, IEF, SHY} (module-level; EW buy-and-hold bar). Event set: every 10y/20y/30y auction (incl. reopenings) from fiscaldata.treasury.gov, admitted only if auction_date − announcement_date ≥ 5 calendar days (PIT-clean). PRE = the 5 sessions strictly before a qualifying auction. Weights set at close for next session: PRE → (EDV 0, IEF 1/3, SHY 2/3); else → (EDV **0.600**, IEF 1/3, SHY 0.067). **w_hi=0.600 is a FIXED constant, not fitted** (chosen for duration-neutrality on average; breakeven flat 19.3–20.0 across 0.50–0.70). Hold declared 21 days. No shorts, no leverage; whole shares suffice at $2,000 NAV. At most one state transition per session — no same-session collision, asserted in code.

**18.5-year result:** strategy +9.41%/yr vs bar +2.14%/yr; **ACTIVE +6.95%/yr, TE 6.79%, IR +1.02, t +4.40**; breakeven **19.7 bps/side**; positive **17 of 19 years**; bond-up years +6.62% vs bond-down +6.98% (direction-dependence neutralised).
